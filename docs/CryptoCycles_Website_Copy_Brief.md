## 5. Replacement copy — screens

Replace the eight-panel grid with the screens the app actually has.

### Home / cycle chart

> Shows the day count since the last Bitcoin halving and overlays how 2016 and 2020 moved on the same day-count, on a log scale. It is a historical overlay. It does not draw a future path or name a top.

> A searchable list of catalog coins with the last live print. If the feed is down, the app shows a dash — not a made-up sparkline.

> A reading of recent daily closes (and funding or ETF prints when those feeds are live). This is tape reading, not a forecast, and not a buy or sell call.

### Futures

### Macro

## 8. Replacement copy — FAQ

**What is CryptoCycles?**  
An Android app for historical Bitcoin cycle context, live market prints, and blockchain education. It does not tell you what to buy or sell.

**Is CryptoCycles an exchange?**  
No. You cannot buy, sell, or swap crypto in the app.

**Is CryptoCycles a wallet?**  
No. It does not hold keys, coins, or customer funds.

**Does CryptoCycles execute trades?**  
No. It does not place, route, or execute trades and does not connect to your exchange account.

**What does the cycle chart show?**  
The day count since the last halving and how earlier cycles looked on the same day-count. It is history, not a projected future and not a top call.

**What is tape reading?**  
A description of recent daily closes and any live funding or ETF print. It is not a forecast.

**What is the AI?**  
An assistant that reads the on-screen snapshot only. Not a financial advisor. Not a peak predictor.

**Are there whale alerts or a signal log?**  
No. Those were old site copy. The live app does not sell whale radar or a forward signal log.

**How should historical cycle data be read?**  
As context. Past paths can differ. Nothing here is financial advice.

**Where do I download it?**  
Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aistudio.cryptocycles.app
